Privacy Policy

App: Spectrum Analyzer – Audio FFT
Developer: Josef Dave Reyes Llorente
Effective date: August 20, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Josef Dave Reyes Llorente (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the iOS application Spectrum Analyzer – Audio FFT (the “App”).

The App provides real-time spectrum analysis using the device microphone, on-device FFT processing (roughly 20 Hz–20 kHz visualization), peak frequency detection, an approximate audio input level meter (not a calibrated professional SPL meter), freeze/pause of the live spectrum display, and an optional one-time Pro unlock via Apple In-App Purchase for higher FFT resolution.

No user account or login is required. The App does not operate developer-owned backend servers, cloud sync, or a developer-owned database for user content.

Information We Collect

What we do not collect. We do not collect personal information through developer-operated servers for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking. The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash SDKs beyond what may be provided by Apple as part of iOS and the App Store. We do not sell personal data. We do not offer social features, user accounts, cloud sync, or developer-side storage of microphone audio.

What may be processed on your device or by Apple.

  • Microphone audio is processed locally on your device for live spectrum and related on-device displays.
  • Local settings, if any, remain on your device.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and terms.

We do not receive microphone audio uploads to developer servers; the App has no developer-operated servers for that purpose.

Microphone / Audio Data

The App requires microphone permission for real-time analysis.

Audio is processed on-device for spectrum visualization, peak frequency readout, and an approximate input-level meter. Spectrum/FFT results are generated and shown on the device.

As designed:

  • Audio is not recorded and saved to disk by the App.
  • Audio is not imported or exported as audio files by the App.
  • Microphone audio is not shared with or uploaded to the developer.
  • The App does not provide Bluetooth accessory analysis as a product feature.
  • The App does not claim professional certified sound-level calibration.

Microphone input is used for transient analysis only; it is not treated as a stored content library.

Purchases (Apple)

Optional Pro features may be unlocked through a one-time In-App Purchase via Apple StoreKit. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple. We do not operate a separate payment processor for the App.

Apple’s Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

How Information Is Used

On-device microphone processing is used solely to provide App functionality (live spectrum analysis and related on-device readouts/controls). Local settings, if any, are used to remember preferences on the device.

We do not use microphone audio for advertising, tracking across apps or websites, or building developer-side user profiles, because that audio is not sent to developer servers.

Data Sharing / Selling

We do not sell personal data.

We do not share microphone audio with third parties through developer-operated transmission, because the App does not upload that audio to developer servers.

Apple may process information related to App Store distribution and In-App Purchases under Apple’s policies. This App does not use third-party advertising identifiers for ads.

Data Retention

Because microphone audio is processed for real-time on-device analysis and is not uploaded to developer servers, we do not retain microphone audio on developer systems.

Local settings, if any, remain on your device until you delete the App or clear app data via iOS controls. Purchase records associated with your Apple ID are retained by Apple according to Apple’s practices; we do not operate a separate purchase database for the App.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not require account creation. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers.

If you have concerns, contact us at josefdave.llorente@gmail.com. Given the App’s design (no developer backend for user content), device-local data is generally addressed by removing the App or changing microphone permission in iOS Settings.

International Users

The App processes microphone audio on the device where the App is installed. We do not operate developer servers that receive that audio. Apple may process App Store and purchase-related data as described in Apple’s privacy materials.

This policy does not assert specific GDPR legal bases, data-protection officer appointments, or EU representative details.

Your Choices

  • Microphone permission: Allow or deny access in iOS Settings. Without microphone access, analysis features that require the microphone will not function.
  • Purchases / restore: Manage and restore eligible purchases through Apple’s In-App Purchase / App Store mechanisms.
  • Deletion: Delete the App to remove it and any on-device local settings associated with it, subject to how iOS manages app data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised when changes are posted. When this page is updated, use the same URL in App Store Connect.
